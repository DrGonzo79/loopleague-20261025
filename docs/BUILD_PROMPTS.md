# Phased build prompts

## Phase 1 — demand instrumentation

“Add a private buyer-test workspace that records agency interview evidence, explicit budget response, intro source, and next step. Do not add CRM breadth. Include CSV export and tests for the kill criteria.”

## Phase 2 — paid micro-season

“Replace demo fixtures with Postgres-backed seasons, schools, faculty verification, consent evidence references, and rule-versioned rankings. Use faculty-mediated invitations and signed upload URLs. Add audit logs, deletion workflow, role permissions, and integration tests.”

## Phase 3 — safe media operations

“Add asynchronous media transcoding and a human moderation queue. Permit model-assisted safety flags only as recommendations. Measure false positives/negatives, require reviewer attribution, and block publication until consent and safety reviews pass.”

## Phase 4 — sponsor reporting

“Generate a rights-aware sponsor recap from approved clips and verified metrics. Include cohort retention, school completion, incident summary, and cost per approved clip. Never expose student PII or unapproved media.”
